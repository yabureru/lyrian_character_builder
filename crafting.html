How crafting works

  • Crafting is an Interlude action (1 IP). You pay the materials up front; if you finish short of the item's Crafting HP, the craft fails and the materials are lost.
  • Basic Craft (1 die, can repeat): add a check. Steady Craft (1 die, once): the die counts as a 5. Beginner's Luck (1 die, once): roll 2d10 and keep the higher, no skill added. Standard Finish (2 dice, once): double your points and stop; not allowed if you're adding a mod or a better material.
  • Mods cost crafting points on top of the item's Crafting HP (you need Crafting HP + mod points). Generic rerolls (like the Human racial) don't work; crafting-class abilities do.
  • Alchemy always makes 3 of the item; the listed materials already cover all 3.
  • The chance shown assumes you pick the best action each time after seeing your rolls.
