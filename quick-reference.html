Stats and what they roll

Crafting skills (Alchemy, Armorsmithing, Artificing, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Culinary, Farming...) and gathering skills (Foraging, Mining...) have no stat: just the skill points plus expertise.

Skill checks

DCDifficulty
10Initiate: someone with basic training
15Professional: someone who does it for a living
20Expert: years of training
25Specialist: devoted to the field
30Master: a life's work
  • Expertise is a narrow specialty under a skill (Jumping under Athletics). Each skill point can become 2 expertise points. Only the best one applies to a roll.
  • Caps: 15 points per skill and per expertise; +5 at 5,000 Spirit Core; no cap at 10,000. Crafting skills cap at 10. Bonuses from items and abilities ignore caps.
  • Skill encounters: events have HP and a Hazard counter. A successful Skill Check deals 1 damage to the event, and that base skill is then used up against it. The event clears at 0 HP; it fails when the Hazard counter hits 0.
  • Group Stealth while travelling: average of the highest and lowest rolls. Stealthy travel is at half speed.

Your turn

  • A round is about 10 seconds. You get 4 AP every turn.
  • RP (reactions) only refills at the start of combat, not every turn. Spend it carefully.
  • Reactions resolve last in, first out. An action that gets cancelled is refunded.
  • Each ability once per round, unless it's Rapid (upgrades like Fireball II count as the same ability).
  • Only one Secret Art or Esoteric Art per encounter. One Encounter Start ability each.
  • Moving costs 1 AP per Speed's worth of distance. Difficult terrain costs double. You can't end in an occupied space.
  • Leaving an enemy's reach provokes an opportunity attack; forced movement doesn't.
  • Ranged attacks (and Spells) provoke opportunity attacks from adjacent enemies unless they're Safe.
  • Escaping: start your turn on the map edge and spend all your AP.
  • Getting a non-combat item out of a pack costs 4 AP in combat.
Surprise: the side that's unaware is surprised; everyone not surprised gets +1 Initiative, and whoever triggered the fight goes first.

Attacking

  1. Check range and pay the cost.
  2. Roll accuracy against Evasion.
  3. Roll damage.
  4. Defender may Dodge (1 RP), Block (1 RP) or do nothing.
  5. Guard reduces the damage (minimum 1, unless Blocking).
  6. Damage reduction, then temp HP, then HP.
  7. Other effects.
  • Critical hit: a natural 20 that hits deals maximum damage and gains Half Pierce. Blocking targets can't be crit. Katanas crit on 19-20; Expose lowers the threshold.
  • On hit = the accuracy roll succeeded. On damage = it dealt more than 0 damage.
  • True damage ignores Guard, shields and temp HP. Full Pierce ignores Guard. Half Pierce ignores Guard unless the target Dodges or Blocks.
  • Hidden attacker: your first attack (2 AP or less) gets Trick Attack (the target pays double RP to react).
  • Areas: you're caught if at least half of one of your squares is covered.
  • Dual wield (two one-handed weapons): once per turn, after a basic Light attack, a free Light attack with the other weapon.

  • Temp HP is lost first and doesn't stack (the higher amount wins). It doesn't stop "on damage" effects.
  • Guard lowers each hit by its value, to a minimum of 1 damage (Blocking can reduce it further).
  • Rooted characters Dodge at 13 + Agility. Over your Burden limit, you're Rooted.

Downed, injuries and after the fight

  • 0 HP: Downed. You're Prone, every attack hits you, Guard is 0, and you can only use Downed actions.
  • Below negative max HP: Mortal Wound. You can't act; you die at the end of the encounter unless it's removed.
  • Grunts die at 0 HP.

Encounter conclusion (in order)

  1. One conclusion action each: Second Wind (heal 4d4) · Draw Mana (+1) · Gather Parts (Survival DC 15 grunt / 20 heroic / 25 boss) · Scavenge · Treat Conditions (with a kit).
  2. Deaths: Mortal Wounds die; anyone else Downed comes back at 1 HP.
  3. Injuries: if you were Downed, roll a d10 once on the injury table (hand, RP, mana, speed, AP, eye...).
  4. Loot, then EXP.

Resting and food

Rest (8 hours)HP regained
On the floor1 × Toughness
Camp or basic lodging2 × Toughness
Luxury lodging3 × Toughness
  • Mana comes from food: eat once to regain Mana, then not again until you've rested. Snack 1 · basic meal or ration 2 · gourmet meal 3 · legendary food 4.
  • One rest action per person per rest; most need a kit (Medical Kit, a set-up Camping Kit...).
  • Food: 40 food units (or 1 ration) a day. Each day without enough: a starvation level (−5 Burden limit, −25% travel speed, all stats −1).
  • During a rest you can spend EXP on skills and abilities, but not new classes (those need an Interlude).
Mirane: one tier lower. No camping kit: no HP. Camping kit: 1 × Toughness. Rations and a basic culinary kit restore only 1 Mana. Food units cost 0.5 Clim each.

Interlude (downtime)

  • IP from the GM: none / 1 (days) / 2 (weeks) / 3 (months). IP doesn't carry over. Each IP also gives 1 Errand Point (the first errand is free).
  • Learn Class: 1 IP + tier × 100 EXP. Each later step costs 100 EXP.
  • Train: +25 EXP · Job: 300 Clim (+300 with a mastered crafting class) · Gather: 600 units (double with the mastered tier 1 gathering class)
  • Rest: start the next adventure with +20% HP and Mana as temp pools · Recover: remove one injury · Heal Injury (someone else's)
  • Crafting, Appraise Item, Open Shop, Mentor Class, Research, Investigate, and class actions that cost IP.
Mirane: 1 IP each Wednesday while in town (not on expedition); spend it before the next reset or before you leave. Job 150 Clim (+50 with a mastered artisan class). Gather 300 units (+300 with the tier 1 gathering class). Expeditions give 75 EXP a week, journals up to 25 more. Using natural IP on an IP action gives 20 EXP (once a week).

Damage types

  • Physical: slashing, piercing, bludgeoning
  • Poison
  • Magic: Arcane, Fire, Water, Earth (Acid), Wind, Lightning, Frost
  • Divine: Holy, Dark (Necrotic)
  • Astra: rare, mostly from Fiends

Immunity to a main type covers its subtypes, but not the other way round.

From the 84 official monster stat blocks (v0.13.2, read 2026-10-28). Base numbers before any GM changes. "Typical" = the middle value.

  • Can't be hit: a hit needs total ≥ Evasion and the best roll is 20, so while you Dodge you're safe once your Dodge evasion ≥ 21 + their accuracy: 24 beats typical grunts, 26 every grunt, 27 heroics, 28 bosses. Armor lowers Dodge; Sure Hit, Lock On and area attacks get past it.
  • Can't miss: a Precise attack (d20 + 2 × Focus) hits a non-dodging enemy on a 1 once 1 + 2 × Focus ≥ its Evasion: Focus 5 covers typical grunts and bosses, Focus 6 every listed enemy except one slime (Evasion 15).
  • Light attacks to 1 damage: Guard 13 vs every grunt, Guard 16 vs every heroic and boss (Light attack = 2d4 + Power). Heavy attacks average 21 (grunts) to 27 (bosses).
  • Saves (2d10 + Toughness vs your Potency 11 + Focus): 2d10 rolls 11+ about 55% of the time, 14+ 28%, 17+ 10%, 20 only 1%.
  • Initiative: enemies are mostly +1 to +6 (outliers: Frost Maiden +10, Ice Queen +11 with Frosty Start). You always go first once your bonus is 3 more than theirs.

Keywords

Every keyword from the official keyword list, in plain words.

Active
An ability with this keyword can't be the target of a Prepare action.
Aid
Can only target allies, unless the ability says you can target yourself.
Artillery
Can hit units that aren't hidden even behind cover (including full cover). Cover is judged from above the target, not from the attacker.
Augment
An attack already changed by an Augment effect can't get a second Augment.
Blanketing
Against several targets you make one accuracy roll and one damage roll, and both apply to every target.
Bleeding
Take 1d6 true damage at the start of your turn.
Blinded
-X to accuracy rolls (X = Blinded value). At 3 or more: no ranged attacks unless you've found the target some way other than sight. At 5 or more: your attacks can't get Sure Hit either.
Block Crush
Ignores the extra Guard a target gets from taking the Block action.
Broken
Only applies to equipment. Broken armor or shields give no bonus and no penalty. A broken weapon can only be used as an improvised weapon.
Burning
Take X damage at the end of your turn. Spend 1 AP to remove it.
Calcification
Your Agility is lowered by 1.
Challenge
If a challenged character attacks without including the challenger as a target, the challenger may make a melee attack of opportunity against it. No attack of opportunity if the challenged character is itself making an attack of opportunity, or if the challenger can't be targeted (for example because of Charm).
Charm
You can't willingly attack whoever charmed you, unless the only other valid targets are your allies. Area effects that hit allies also hit the charmer; area effects that skip allies also skip the charmer.
Circuit
The first time each combat you use a Circuit ability, it costs 1 less mana. After that, the discount is gone for all your Circuit abilities.
Combo
Gets an extra bonus when used right after a different ability. The bonus lasts until you spend AP on something that isn't a Combo ability.
Concentration
Only one Concentration effect can be active at a time. You can end it on your own turn for 0 AP. You lose it if you're Downed.
Crushing
If the attack hits and isn't Blocked, it deals at least damage equal to your Power.
Daze
You lose 1 AP right away. If you have no AP left, you regain 1 less AP at the start of your next turn instead. A Dazed target can't be Dazed again until the end of its next turn.
Disarm
You can't make weapon attacks with the weapon you were holding when Disarmed. When the Disarm ends you must draw that weapon again to use it. Unarmed attacks aren't affected.
Divine
If you have the Divine's Chosen breakthrough, this deals your chosen divine's damage type instead of Holy.
Domain
A spell that creates a domain area. Only one domain area can be active at a single location at a time.
Dual Wield
Using it uses up your dual wield attack for the round. Can't be used if you've already made a dual wield attack this round. After using it you can't make another dual wield attack that round.
Enchantment
Applied to a weapon; a weapon can hold only one enchantment at a time. On a channeling weapon, the enchantment also applies to spells you cast while holding it.
Esoteric Art
Only one Esoteric Art or Secret Art ability per encounter.
Expose
Lowers the target's crit threshold by X (Expose 1: crits on 19-20). Stacks with katanas and other abilities that lower the attacker's crit threshold. Removed once the target is critically hit, even if it defended.
Fire
Fire damage can set unattended, easily burned objects alight.
Flashback
Refers back to an earlier moment in the campaign: planning your character did in advance to set up the current situation.
Full Pierce
Damage can't be reduced by Guard.
Half Pierce
Ignores Guard unless the target Blocks or Dodges.
Half Rapid
Can be used on each creature only once per round.
Hellfire
Like Burning: set damage at the end of the target's turn. If it isn't removed before the end of the target's next turn, it can't be removed the normal way any more. Abilities can still dispel it. Creatures immune to Burning can still get Hellfire, and for them it stays removable the normal way even after a turn. Ends automatically when the encounter ends.
Hiding
Enemies can't see or target you, and can't pinpoint where you are even if they know you're near. Lost when you move, attack, or use an ability without the Stealth keyword, unless that action says otherwise.
Illumination
The next attack against you gets +X accuracy. Used up whether that attack hits or misses.
Immovable
While not flying, other creatures' knockdowns and forced movement (knockbacks, pulls, etc.) don't affect you.
Instant
Can be used in the middle of another action, pausing it. Resolves right away, before the interrupted action finishes. If the interrupted action's target becomes invalid, that action is cancelled and its cost refunded.
Isolated
You're Isolated when no allies are within 10ft and 2+ enemies (or 1 boss) are within 10ft.
Killing Blow
Triggers when you bring an enemy to 0 HP or less. The same Killing Blow effect can only be gained once from a given target.
Lock On
Ignores the bonus a target gets from Dodging.
Lockstep
Always counts as meeting the target's base Evasion (with its bonuses); the target can still Dodge normally.
Mandate
Your next single-target attack must target whoever gave you the Mandate; otherwise you've broken the mandate. Challenge doesn't work on you while you have Mandate.
Miracle
Each character can use only one Miracle per encounter.
Mystic Eye
The ability is used through a Mystic Eye.
Ninjutsu
Needs both hands free. Holding a small weapon, Saboteur thread dagger, one-handed thrown weapon or set of missiles still counts as a free hand.
Offstep
Attacks against you always count as meeting your base Evasion (with its bonuses); you can still Dodge normally.
Opener
Can only be used as the first action of your turn. Paying upkeep isn't an action, so it doesn't count.
Opportunistic
Can be used instead of a normal attack of opportunity. It still counts as your attack of opportunity, so you give up the normal one.
Overcharge
Pay extra AP and/or Mana to make the ability stronger.
Pass
During this movement you can move through enemy spaces.
Persist
Removing it costs X extra mana; if the remover isn't using mana, X extra AP (or RP) instead. The extra cost doesn't apply to the effect's normal removal method, if it has one (e.g. 1 AP to clear Burning).
Petrify
+10 Guard, but every attack against you hits automatically and you can't take actions. Allies can use you as cover; if an attack would have missed them because of that cover, it hits you instead. Normal "remove negative effects" abilities don't work; only effects that name Petrify remove it.
Pinpoint
Your attacks ignore an amount of the target's Guard equal to your Focus. Guard can't go below 0 from this.
Presence Concealment
Hides your mana output and aura: your Spirit Core counts as 0 for detection and aura abilities. +5 Stealth.
Prone
Abilities with this keyword can be used while Prone with no penalty. While Prone, attacks without this keyword cost +1 AP and +1 RP.
Quick
After you use a Quick ability, the next Quick ability you use that turn costs +1 AP.
Rage
Can be used while you're in Rage Stance.
Rapid
Can be used more than once per turn.
Regrowth
At the start of your turn, heal 3 + the Focus of whoever applied it.
Root
You can't move. When you Dodge, your Evasion is 13 + Agility (rounded down).
Safe
Doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity just for being a spell or a ranged attack.
Salvo
All attacks in the Salvo resolve at the same time. One basic Dodge or Block against one of them covers the rest for no extra cost. Extra effects tied to that defense (like healing from a Block) happen only once. The Salvo provokes only one attack of opportunity.
Sanctify
When an attack hits the Sanctified creature, the attacker heals 3 + the applier's Focus. Each trigger removes one stack. An attacker already at full HP can choose not to trigger it.
Scatter
Against several targets, roll accuracy and damage separately for each one.
Secret Art
Only one Secret Art or Esoteric Art ability per encounter.
Setup
Finished by spending AP on your next turn. While charging you can't take other AP actions, and attacks against you gain Trick Attack. You can abandon it instead of finishing; the leftover AP stays unspent. You can cancel it any time for 0 RP. It can't trigger until your next turn starts, and Setup abilities can't be Prepared. Starting the charge costs at least 1 AP.
Shaken
Maximum AP lowered by 1.
Shieldbreaker
Double damage to Shield HP and Temporary HP. Damage that carries over past them isn't doubled.
Sickened
Rolls that use Focus, Power, Agility or Toughness treat that stat as 1 lower.
Sidestep
This movement doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity.
Slow
Your movement speed is halved.
Soaked
Take X extra damage from Lightning damage.
Song
Only one Song can be active at a time.
Sound
The effect is sound-based; anyone who can't hear it is unaffected.
Spell
Counts as a spell and provokes attacks of opportunity unless something says otherwise.
Stacking
These abilities give stacks of something. You can only hold one kind of stack from them at a time; gaining a different kind removes all your old stacks.
Stagger
Evasion lowered by X.
Stance
Only one Stance can be active at a time.
Static
At the end of your next turn, you deal X lightning damage to each ally within 5ft of you.
Stealth
Doesn't break Hiding. You still need to be in low cover or concealment afterward to stay hidden.
Strafe
You must move at least the Strafe distance toward the target before making this attack.
Stun
Your AP, AP recovery and RP all become 0. When it ends you get back the unspent RP it took.
Summon
Counts toward your summon limit: at most 2 summons at once. A summon can't be the target of abilities that need a willing ally.
Sunder
Guard lowered by X.
Sure Hit
Always hits with no accuracy roll, but automatically misses a target in full cover.
Teleport
You move by teleporting instead of normal movement. Doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity and gets past most roots and grapples. You must be able to see where you're going; otherwise you ignore difficult terrain and solid obstacles. Unless stated, only you and your gear teleport; creatures you're touching stay behind.
Toxic
Take 1d6 Full Pierce Poison damage at the start of your turn.
Transformation
Only one transformation effect can be active at a time.
Trick Attack
The target must pay double the normal RP to react to it.
Type
You can only be one "type" at a time.
Unarmed Strike
Must be made with an unarmed attack.
Unbalanced
Your next attack that uses AP (even a normally 0 AP one) costs 1 extra AP.
Upkeep
At the start of your turn you choose whether to pay; if you don't, the effect ends. You can't pay upkeep while Downed.
Weakened
Your max AP becomes 2 and max RP becomes 1. When it ends you get back the unspent RP it took.
