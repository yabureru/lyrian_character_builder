Combat Sandbox

Lyrian Chronicles · unofficial · rough model for comparing builds Planner Quick reference
How to use: in the planner open Share → Character data, press Copy, and paste the code into Build A (or type the numbers in yourself). Do the same for a second build in Build B to compare, then press Run. Each scenario is fought a few thousand times with random dice.
This is a rough model. It knows your defenses well (Evasion, Dodge, Block, Guard, RP, HP, saves) and shoots two basic attacks a round. It does not know your class abilities, range and cover, healing, initiative or the Downed rules. Read results as "which build is tougher, and by roughly how much", not as exact odds.

Settings

Grabs and control spells root you: Dodge only gives half its bonus until you're free. Party fights split the enemies' attacks across the party and the rest of the party removes enemy HP each round.

Results

Press Run.

Enemies

From about 150 enemy rolls read in 8 recorded sessions (3 GMs, late 2026) and Cola Bear's published enemy docs. Attack = d20 + accuracy against your Evasion (or Dodge when you react).
